How it works

Three seconds from “bored” to “let’s go”.

  1. 01

    Say how long you have

    20 minutes or a whole evening. Add your mood, your budget, whether you can go out, and who’s with you.

  2. 02

    It ranks everything

    Your Steam games, new uploads from your channels, this week’s free Epic games and 95 hand-picked ideas, checked against the clock and the weather.

  3. 03

    Press Start

    It launches the game, opens the video or lays out the plan. Rate it afterwards and the next picks get better.

What it picks from

The stuff you already have, and new things to try.

  • Steam Your library, with the games you actually play near the top.
  • YouTube New videos from your channels and topics, sized to your time.
  • Epic Games Store This week’s free games, before they’re gone.
  • 95 ideas Sport, cooking, learning, making things and slowing down, each with a photo.
  • The weather Outdoor ideas rise when it’s nice out and sink when it rains.

Together

Better with friends.

  • Say “I’m free for 1h30”: friends see it right away.
  • Invite them to any pick. They answer “I’m in” in one tap.
  • Share your Steam list and freetime finds the co-op games you both own.
  • Instant messages, friends added by tag, block and report built in.

All optional: freetime works fully without an account.

FAQ

Questions

Is it really free?

Yes. No subscription, no ads, no in-app purchases. It’s open source under the MIT license.

Windows says “Windows protected your PC”. Is it safe?

That warning appears for new apps that haven’t built a reputation with Microsoft yet. Click “More info”, then “Run anyway”. The installer is built in public by GitHub from the code you can read.

Code signing policy

Do I need Steam or YouTube?

No. Without them you still get 95 ideas, the weather and Epic’s free games. Connect what you use, when you want.

Mac, Linux, phone?

Windows first. Android is next on the list.

How does it update?

When a new version is out, freetime offers to install it. Updates are signed: the app refuses anything we didn’t sign. You can turn the check off in Settings.
